# The checks

Look at one moment of every shot, and at every moment something hits.

- Two eyes and one mouth on every person you can read, and they agree.
- The same person is the same person from shot to shot.
- No word on a face. One word at a time.
- No cut edge showing under a body. No halo round anybody.
- No weapon points at anybody.
- The right number of hands.
- The last line is true, or says "the story goes".

## A second reader

Somebody who has not seen it being made, a fresh agent or a person, lists every problem, numbered by shot. Report the count. Fix one at a
time. Report the count again.

## Changing one shot

Name the shot and change only that. *"Shot 5: shocked from the word scared. Touch nothing else."* When you report it, say whether you did it
with a tool or the person asked and you did it.
