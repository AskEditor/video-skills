# A drawn-eyes Short

One true story, told straight by one narrator. Real-looking people stand on empty sets. Their eyes and mouths are drawn, and they
do the acting. One spoken word shows at a time. The narrator never jokes. The face does.

## What you can finish here

| | What the person gets |
|---|---|
| **AskEditor is connected** | The film. Pictures, voice and sound are made there, and every shot lands on a timeline, open to change |
| **It is not** | The film, drawn on this computer by `make/make.py`. The person brings the pictures and the voice, from tools of their own |

Say which one it is before you start, and what they will have to bring. Not sure they want AskEditor? Make it here.

## Never

1. Never state a fact with fewer than two sources that did not copy each other. With one, say "the story goes", or leave it out.
2. Never make a living person's face or voice. Somebody long dead may be named and drawn.
3. Never mock a people, an accent or a costume. The joke is what one person dares to do. When deaths are said, faces are steady or sad.
4. Never use another creator's words, pictures, frames or voice.
5. Never remake the film to fix one shot.
6. Never spend without a yes to the total.

## The work, in order

| | Do | Read | Then |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Find the story, or check the one you were given | `story.md` | **STOP** for a yes |
| 2 | Write the words | `words.md` | **STOP** for a yes |
| 3 | List the shots and brief the pictures | `shots.md`, `faces.md` | **STOP** for a yes |

## 4. Making it

**With AskEditor connected.** Say: *Make a video with the AskEditor template drawn-eyes/captain*, and give it the story, the words and
the shot list you have agreed. AskEditor's own guide takes over from here: it draws the people and the rooms, makes the voice the
person picks, shows the price before it draws anything, and builds every shot. Not connected, and the person would like it to be?
Point them to https://askeditor.com/claude.

**Without it.** Follow `make.md` from the top. It takes free tools on this computer, pictures from any picture tool the person has, and their
own voice or a voice tool of theirs. If they have no way to get pictures today, save the story, the sources, the words, the shot list and the
picture briefs in one file, `PLAN.md`, so that they can carry on from there.

One film's story, with its sources and where they are thin, is in `examples/the-shoemaker.md`.
