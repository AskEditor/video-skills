# The faces

Every person whose face is big enough to read has two drawn eyes and one drawn mouth, and they agree. An animal may have eyes and no mouth.

- A face changes on the word that causes it, eyes and mouth usually together.
- The mouth does not follow the narration. Nobody lip-syncs.
- Leave a gap between the eyes over the nose. Eyes that touch read as glasses.
- Place the eyes by looking closely at each face, enlarged, then look again. A small mistake reads as a mask.
- Backs, hands, and anybody soft and close to the lens have no face.
- Far people still get eyes, smaller.

Write the feeling in the shot list, one of eight: steady, doubt, shock, smug, cross, sad, joy, asleep. An honest person is never drawn sly.
