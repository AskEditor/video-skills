# The words

- Open on an action, in its first second. The strangest thing in the story. No greeting and no date.
- Say one new thing in each sentence. Short sentences, and the word that matters goes last.
- Keep turning it: something goes wrong, or somebody answers back, again and again.
- Name nobody at the start. Either name the main person part of the way in, or keep the name for the end when the name is the surprise. Choose one.
- Never a question. Never a joke in the narration.
- The last line turns everything before it.
- Beside every line, its two sources. A line with one source says "the story goes".

## How long

Our three films do this. They are three films, not a rule.

| | Length | Words | Shots |
|---|---|---|---|
| The shoemaker | 52 s | 165 | 32 |
| Hannibal | 93 s | 308 | 54 |
| The cobra effect | 84 s | 259 | 54 |

That is a little over three words a second, and a new shot about every second and a half. Make about fifty seconds unless the person asks for longer.

## The voice, when there is one

One narrator, one take, dry, at normal speed. Close the pauses. Then read the time of every word: every cut in the film comes from those times.

## STOP

Show the words with their sources. Wait for a yes.
