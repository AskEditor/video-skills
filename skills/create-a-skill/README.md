# Create a skill

Give Claude a video you made, or one you have the right to study. It works out how that kind of video is made, by counting and not by
guessing, and writes it down as a skill of your own. One video is a start. Three or four of the same kind tell the rules from the choices.

**You give it** the files, and the answer to one question: whose are they?

**You get** a `SKILL.md` any agent can follow, with what it cannot do at the top. With a second video of the same kind, also a score for how well it predicts the next.

It takes the craft. It never takes the pictures, the people, the words, the voice or the story.
